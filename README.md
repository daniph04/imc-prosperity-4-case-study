# IMC Prosperity 4 — Strategy Evaluation & Backtesting

An evidence-backed case study from a five-round international trading competition. The work focused on comparing strategy experiments, testing optimistic assumptions, and deciding which ideas still held up after transaction costs.

> **Team result:** qualified for the finals and finished **750th of 18,803 teams worldwide (top 4%)**.

![IMC Prosperity 4 finals qualification](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b5d26d4f-7f62-4793-af1a-732329e15c29)

## My contribution

- Helped direct and compare strategy experiments across five rounds.
- Used Python-based backtests and official run evidence to challenge optimistic local results.
- Tested parameter sensitivity and the effect of transaction costs, including bid-ask spreads.
- In the final round, supported the decision to narrow ten candidate product categories to four that remained viable after costs.
- Documented uncertainty and kept team outcomes separate from my individual contribution.

## What the project demonstrates

- Experimental design and evidence-based iteration
- Python-based analysis and backtesting
- Decision-making under uncertainty
- Knowing when a strategy should not trade
- Team collaboration under a competition deadline
